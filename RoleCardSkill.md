# Travel Guardian Role Card Prompts

This document defines the shared visual system and production prompts for the travel personality quiz character cards.

## Shared Series Direction

- Format: polished 3D animal travel guardian character sheet meets collectible game card.
- Audience: charming for young users while remaining clear, warm, and sophisticated for travelers aged 50-60.
- Rendering: rounded original animal mascot, expressive eyes, tactile fur, premium animation-film quality, warm studio lighting.
- Layout: one large hero pose, front/side/back views, five expression panels, favorite-item close-ups, travel-style icons, ability chart, recommended trip types, and color swatches.
- Background: warm cream card surface, restrained dividers, generous margins, clear hierarchy, no overlapping panels.
- Text: allow one accurate character title and short section headings; minimize tiny body text because production copy will be overlaid in Vue.
- Originality: evoke a premium animated animal world without copying any existing film, studio, character, costume, or trademark.
- Avoid: humans, anime styling, realistic animal photography, aggressive anatomy, dark cyberpunk scenes, harsh neon, real logos, watermarks, clutter, and excessive illegible text.

## LUNA the Cloud Cat

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical collectible character game card and travel personality quiz result artwork

Primary request:
Create LUNA the Cloud Cat, an original 3D animal travel guardian character card. LUNA represents comfortable vacations, slow travel, sea-view hotels, hot springs, spa time, soft pillows, good breakfasts, private transfers, and relaxed family trips.

Character identity:
LUNA the Cloud Cat | Comfort Vacation Type
Role: Comfort Travel Advisor
Personality: elegant, calm, warm, loyal, unhurried, and quietly picky about beds, pillows, breakfast, and room quality.
Signature expression: a serene, gently judgmental look that says, "I have already checked in to life."

Character design:
A round fluffy cat with soft cream-white fur, tiny rounded paws, large gentle expressive eyes, plush cheeks, and a large cloud-shaped tail. The silhouette must feel soft and comforting rather than kitten-like or childish. LUNA wears a sea-salt blue scarf and an oversized hotel keycard charm on a pale-gold strap. Add a small pearl-shell charm and a pillow badge.

Hero pose:
LUNA sits proudly on a small cream suitcase or a cloud-soft hotel pillow. Show relaxed posture, crossed or neatly placed paws, and a calm premium-resort attitude. Include a subtle sea-view suggestion behind the hero pose without turning the card into a full scenic illustration.

Favorite items:
Hotel keycard, cream suitcase, pillow badge, pearl-shell charm, tea cup, room card, soft travel blanket, spa towel.

Expression panels:
Happy comfort face, picky pillow face, sleepy spa face, surprised-by-a-rushed-schedule face, satisfied sea-view face.

Card sections:
VIEWS, EXPRESSIONS, ROLE, PERSONALITY, TRAVEL STYLE, FAVORITE ITEMS, COMFORT LEVEL, RECOMMENDED TRIP TYPE, COLOR PALETTE.

Recommended trip visuals:
Sea-view resort, hot-spring stay, spa weekend, premium hotel escape, relaxed family trip, private-car day tour.

Color palette:
Sea-salt blue #7DB7C5, cream white #F7F2E8, pearl white #FFFDF8, pale gold #D9B56D, soft beige #D9CEC0, warm charcoal #403D3A. Keep the overall mood airy, refined, and sunlit.

Style and lighting:
Polished 3D animation character sheet meets collectible game card, premium rounded mascot, soft tactile fur, warm natural studio lighting, clean cream background, playful but refined, all-ages appeal.

Text:
Prominent title may read "LUNA the Cloud Cat". Use only short, clean English section labels. Keep tiny body copy minimal and prioritize icons and visual panels.

Constraints:
Animal-only original character. Keep the hotel keycard charm and cloud tail clearly visible. Maintain stable panel spacing and a readable hierarchy. Leave enough quiet space for real Chinese UI copy to be added later.

Avoid:
Existing IP, real hotel or travel logos, human character, anime, realistic photography, babyish proportions, sexy styling, dark blue monochrome, harsh neon, messy background, watermark, and excessive tiny text.
```

## RIN the Neon Fox

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical collectible character game card and travel personality quiz result artwork

Primary request:
Create RIN the Neon Fox, an original 3D animal travel guardian character card. RIN represents independent cafes, exhibitions, design shops, interesting neighborhoods, urban walking, hidden alleys, local architecture, street corners, and small galleries.

Character identity:
RIN the Neon Fox | City Explorer Type
Role: City Inspiration Advisor
Personality: intelligent, observant, agile, curious, socially warm, and skilled at discovering quietly excellent local places.
Signature expression: a knowing, slightly playful look that says, "I know there is something good down that alley."

Character design:
A small, softly rounded fox with an agile silhouette but no sharp or predatory features. Use mist-silver-gray and warm cream-white fur, large expressive eyes, rounded paws, and a friendly intelligent face. The ear tips glow softly coral orange. The large fluffy tail contains elegant luminous city-route lines in ticket yellow, coral-orange transfer nodes, and only tiny sky-blue highlights. RIN wears a deep-indigo scarf, a compact vintage-style camera, a visible metro ticket, and a small crossbody bag. RIN holds a coral-orange city notebook.

Hero pose:
RIN stands beside or lightly leans against a refined city wayfinding post, notebook in one paw and camera visible. The pose should feel confident, observant, and ready to turn into a promising side street.

Favorite items:
Compact camera, metro ticket, coral city notebook, takeaway coffee cup, folded city map, crossbody bag, exhibition ticket.

Expression panels:
Hidden-gem discovery face, focused map-reading face, delighted cafe face, suspicious tourist-trap face, satisfied night-walk face.

Card sections:
VIEWS, EXPRESSIONS, ROLE, PERSONALITY, TRAVEL STYLE, FAVORITE ITEMS, CITY SENSE, EXPLORATION LEVEL, RECOMMENDED TRIP TYPE, COLOR PALETTE.

Recommended trip visuals:
City break, cafe-hopping weekend, gallery route, design-district walk, hidden-neighborhood exploration, local-living experience.

Color psychology and palette:
Deep indigo #18324A for intelligence and urban confidence. Coral orange #F27D68 for cafe culture, social warmth, and surprise. Ticket yellow #F2C45A for discovery, wayfinding, and action. Electronic sky blue #58C7E8 only for small inspiration highlights. Mist silver gray #DCE2DF and warm cream white #F7F2E8 for fur and card foundation.

Color placement:
Use deep indigo on the scarf, camera strap, crossbody bag, headings, and structural details. Use coral orange on the notebook, ear-tip glow, selected signs, and key UI highlights. Use ticket yellow on the tail route lines, metro ticket, route markers, arrows, and discovery icons. Do not allow a sage, mint, green, or teal cast in the fur, clothing, tail glow, panels, or overall atmosphere.

Style and lighting:
Polished 3D animation character sheet meets collectible game card, premium rounded mascot, tactile soft fur, bright warm studio lighting, clean cream card background, sophisticated all-ages appeal. The mood is a warm urban treasure hunt, never dark cyberpunk.

Text:
Prominent title may read "RIN the Neon Fox". Use only short, clean English section labels. Keep tiny body copy minimal and prioritize icons and visual panels.

Constraints:
Animal-only original character. Keep the camera, notebook, metro ticket, and luminous route-map tail clearly visible. Maintain clear panel spacing and leave quiet space for real Chinese UI copy to be added later.

Avoid:
Existing IP, real transit or cafe logos, human character, anime, realistic animal photography, sharp aggressive fox anatomy, sage green, mint green, dominant teal, green fur cast, dark cyberpunk scenes, harsh neon, watermark, clutter, and excessive tiny text.
```
